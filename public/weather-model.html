02 / THE CORRECTION

Why the first model
made impossible wind.

01

raw × 0.514444

The input was scaled twice.

IGRA already stores wind in tenths of meters per second. Treating that integer as knots made the starting field about 5.14× too fast.

02

u × du/dt × Δt

The update multiplied state.

Wind was multiplied by its tendency on every step, turning normal upper-air flow into values measured in millions of meters per second.

03

u + du/dt × Δt

The corrected relationship.

This page uses properly scaled observations. Future forecast evolution will use an additive, stability-limited time step.
