What this tree does—and does not—claim

The photographs supplied here cover printed pages 2–11. The tree includes direct ancestors and named siblings or children visible on those pages. It does not assume relationships from missing pages or fill gaps with outside sources.

Names, years, and marriages are preserved from the binder, including entries where the original dates do not line up. In particular, the dates given for Michael Lutz and Peter Lutz conflict. The data remains the binder's version, without substituting external corrections.

Original ancestor numbers (#4, #504, etc.) are shown as printed. Extra relatives listed as children are indexed here using internal identifiers beginning with C, solely for organizing the digital file.

For importing into genealogy software, use the separate GEDCOM file provided alongside this page. It contains the same transcribed people and family links.
